              Custom stats

              Text stats show as chips (Spell DC 15). Give a stat a Max and it becomes a bar under the HP bar (Mana 12/20). "Refills every turn" fills it back up when the creature's turn starts – handy for movement.

              🎲 Initiative

              Roll a d20 and type the number you got – your initiative bonus is added for you. Or let the app roll.
